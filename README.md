## 🗂️ Core Topics & Materials Available

### 📊 Micro & Macroeconomics
* Dynamic simulations of nonlinear consumer/producer surplus, deadweight loss, and market structures.
* Structural derivations of macroeconomic frameworks (IS-LM, Keynesian Multiplier Dynamics).

### 🧮 Mathematical Economics
* Matrix algebra applications, Cramer's Rule for market equilibrium, and economic optimizations.
* Visualizing eigenvalues and eigenvectors using applied economic data arrays.

### 📉 Econometrics & Data Simulation
* **Time Series Analysis:** Vector Autoregression (VAR) lag length selections and diagnostic testing.
* **Hypothesis Testing:** Step-by-step programming for Z-tests checking serial correlation and autocorrelation.
